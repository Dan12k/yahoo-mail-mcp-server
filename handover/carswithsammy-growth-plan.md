You're on 25,582 and averaging about 18 new followers a day. Hitting 30k by 31 Dec needs about 94 a day, five times your current rate. That's not happening on organic posts alone. ## Part 3 - Step by step plan

### Step 1 - Fix the leaks (this week, one afternoon)
1. Bio. Rewrite it to say what people get by following. Make the link match too. Something like: "UK car girl - S55 AMG + Polo GTI / Shows, drives and honest opinions / My snow foam 👇". Point the link at the snow foam or a link page with both products.
2. Pins. Pin the 1 Nov Polo post, "S Class Perfection" and your best Switzerland post. Those are your best converter, your most shared post and your best-liked post.
3. Highlights. Put the Polo and S55 highlights first and give them clear covers. New visitors should see the two cars in one second.
4. Hashtag typos. Edit #volksvagen to #volkswagen and #contentcreater to #contentcreator on the posts that have them.
5. Giveaway. Right now the only way to enter is to buy something. Under UK rules a prize draw needs a free way to enter, or it can count as an illegal lottery. Ask Splash to add a free route (for example follow both accounts and tag a mate). And reword it so it sounds like you, not the brand.
6. Stop posting "hi" intro reels. Three tries, no traction.

### Step 2 - The weekly posting rhythm (from 16 Nov)
Aim for 4 or 5 feed posts a week. No gaps longer than 2 days. When you went quiet from 28 to 31 Oct, follows dropped to 6 to 9 a day.

Monday: Polo story carousel. Two or three lines about owning it, plus one easy question. This is your conversion post.
Wednesday: S55 reel built for shares. Engine or exhaust sound in the first second, on-screen text hook, under 10 seconds.
Thursday or Friday: S55 carousel. Best photos first, then a strong opinion in the caption that people will want to send to a mate.
Weekend: a Parked Up episode or event reel from a show you're at.
Stories most days. Polls ("keep or sell?"), behind the scenes of shoots, replies to comments. Stories keep the people you just gained.

### Step 3 - The caption formula (every single post)
One specific opinion or mini story, then one question someone can answer in five words. Never a caption that's only a hashtag or emoji. Your ready-made captions from the handover all follow this: "would you map it or leave it", "which car did you sell that you still think about", "daily it or weekends only". Use them.

Then reply to every comment in the first hour. Comments that turn into conversations are why the 1 Nov post kept travelling.

### Step 4 - Fix the reels
Your watch time is 4 to 7.5 seconds and up to three quarters of people skip in the first 3 seconds. So:
1. Open on the most exciting second, like a cold start, a flyby or a revving V8. No intro or build-up.
2. Put a text hook on screen from frame one, like "The car I'll never sell" or "Rate this S55 out of 10".
3. Keep S55 sound clips under 10 seconds so they loop.
4. Event reels hold people longest (7.5 seconds at Lanzante), so keep doing shows. Open each one on the best car, not the car park.
5. Use Instagram's Trial Reels option to test reels on non-followers first. Only share the winners to your main feed.

### Step 5 - Launch Parked Up properly
Make Parked Up a named series with the same opening shot, the same title card and an episode number. A series gives people a reason to follow instead of just like.

Episode 1 at your next show. Use the caption "Walked past everything else at [show] to get to this. Daily it or weekends only?" Then plan the next two weeks of posts around it.

### Step 6 - Brands and collabs
Use Instagram Collab posts with event organisers. You already tagged Lanzante and Duke of London. Next time, invite them as collaborators so the post shows on their page as well. That's free reach to exactly the right audience.

Grow Splash into a proper deal. You have a product with them already. Go back with your numbers: 25.6k followers, 52% UK, posts reaching up to 38.9k people. Ask for a monthly content retainer or a better affiliate cut rather than one-off giveaways.

UK car care brands to pitch. All of these need checking that they're active with creators before you message them: Auto Finesse (Telford), Autobrite Direct (snow foams and coatings), Absolutewax (listed as open to ambassador and affiliate deals) and Clean Me (on-demand detailing). Pitch your angle: a woman who actually details and owns her own performance cars, with an audience that's 72% men aged 18 to 44. Avoid anything that competes directly with your own snow foam.

Your OEM retrofits highlight is a ready-made pitch to retrofit and parts suppliers for the Polo. Offer an install video in return for the part.

Collab with other female car owners. Joint posts and "my car vs her car" content builds the woman-in-a-male-space angle without preaching about it.

Before any pitch, put together a one-page media kit. It needs follower count, audience split, your top 3 posts with reach and the brands you've already worked with.

### Step 7 - Measure it every Monday
The weekly report will score you on three numbers:
1. Follows per 1,000 reach on carousels. Now about 2 on average, target 4 or more.
2. Reel skip rate. Now 43% to 75%, target under 50%.

## Part 4 - Trial Reels (added 14 Nov)

### What they are
A Trial Reel is shown to non-followers only. It stays off her grid and out of her followers' feeds. It sits in the "Drafts and trial reels" section of her profile, which only she can see. After roughly 24 hours she can see how it's doing. She can then share it to everyone by hand, or turn on auto-share so Instagram shares it if it does well in the first 72 hours. She needs a public professional account with 1,000+ followers, which she has.

### Why it suits her
Her reels lose 43% to 75% of viewers in the first 3 seconds. Her talking reels got 196 to 1,185 reach. Trial Reels let her test openings and new formats on strangers, so a flop never shows up in her followers' feeds or on her grid.

### Her benchmarks from the last 30 days
Average watch time 3.8 to 7.5 seconds. Skip rate 43% to 75%. The S55 reels reached about 8,000 people each, with about 50 shares.
A trial counts as a winner if it gets under 50% skip rate, over 6 seconds average watch time, and shares pulling ahead of likes.

### How to post one
Make the reel as normal. On the final share screen, switch on "Trial" before posting. Leave auto-share off for the first couple of weeks so she sees every result herself. Check it after 24 hours, then decide at 48 to 72 hours.

### The testing method
Change one thing at a time. Same clip, different first second or on-screen hook. Run 3 trials a week alongside normal posting.

### First two weeks of tests
Week 1, S55 sound clip, same footage with 3 different hooks:
1. "Rate this sound out of 10"
2. "A supercharged V8 in a limo. Yes really"
3. "The most underrated AMG ever made?"

Week 2:
1. Polo GTI B-road clip: "Is this the best hot hatch you can daily?"
2. Aura snow foam on the black S55, opening on the foam hitting the paint. Keep #ad in the caption.
3. Talking reel: "Things I've been told at car shows that I'm still thinking about." Open on the most outrageous one, not on "hi".

### After each trial
Share the winners to followers. Leave the losers as trials, since nobody but non-followers ever saw them. Write down the winning hook and reuse that style. Check whether the reel brought in follows by looking at new followers on the day it was shared.
